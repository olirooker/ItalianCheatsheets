## Starting a conversation

**Come stai?**  
How are you?

**Come va?**  
How's it going?

**Come va oggi?**  
How’s it going today?

**Che fai di bello?**  
What are you up to?

**Bene!**  
Good!

**Così così**  
So so.

~~**Non molto bene.**~~  
~~Not very good.~~

**Sto molto bene, grazie**  
I'm very well, thank you


## Getting to know someone

**Come ti chiami?**  
What's your name?

**Piacere!**  
Nice to meet you!

**Di dove sei?**  
Where are you from?

**Abiti qui?**  
Do you live here?



## Talking about yourself

**Mi chiamo…**  
My name is...

**Sono di…**  
I'm from...

**Abito a…**  
I live in...

**Studio italiano.**  
I study italian.

**Parlo poco italiano**  
I speak a little italian.

**Nel tempo libero mi piace…**  
In my free time I like to...

**Ho...**  
I have...

**Vado a...**  
I have to go...

**Vorrei...**  
I would like...

**Mi piace...**  
I like...

**Devo / Posso / Voglio**  
I must / can / want



## Keeping the conversation going

**E tu?**  
And you?

**Che bello!**  
That's great!

**Davvero?**  
Really?

**Cosa ne pensi?**  
What do you think?

**Ti piace?**  
Do you like it?

**Anch'io!**  
Me too!

**Non lo so.**  
I don't know.

**Forse.**  
Maybe.



## Understanding and clarifying

**Puoi ripetere, per favore?**  
Can you repeat, please?

**Puoi parlare più lentamente?**  
Can you speak more slowly?

**Non capisco.**  
I don’t understand.

**Capisco.**  
I understand

**Cosa significa…?**  
What does … mean?

**Come si dice … in italiano?**  
How do you say … in Italian?

**Come si scrive ...?**  
How do you write ...?



## Sharing opinions and reactions

**Mi piace molto!**  
I really like it!

**Non mi piace.**  
I don’t like it.

**Va bene!**  
Okay!

**Perfetto!**  
Perfect!
